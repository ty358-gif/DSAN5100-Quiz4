U.S. developers report about $58K more per year than developers in Canada or Australia

Average annual compensation by country, with 95% confidence intervals (US$ thousands).

What I found and why it matters

Among developers who reported a salary, the U.S. average was about $157K a year (median $145K), compared with about $98K in Canada (median $87K) and $99K in Australia (median $96K). The U.S. gap is roughly $57K–$59K, and statistical tests found it clearly larger than chance would explain. Canada and Australia, by contrast, were only about $2K apart, which the tests could not tell from zero. If you are comparing opportunities, this suggests the U.S. market is the outlier, but the numbers are a starting point, not an offer letter.

How people work also differs by country

Share of respondents who work remotely, hybrid or in person. The mix differs across the three countries (chi-square test, p < 0.001); the chart shows how, not why.

How I know, and what it does not tell us

I used 5,415 survey respondents in these three countries who reported an annual salary, compared the averages with Welch’s ANOVA (F(2, 1149) = 502, p < 0.001), and used Games–Howell tests to see which countries differ. Limitations: (1) respondents chose to take the survey and many did not report pay, so they are not a random sample of all developers; (2) salaries are in US dollars with no cost-of-living adjustment, and a difference in average pay does not mean that moving to another country would cause a higher salary.
